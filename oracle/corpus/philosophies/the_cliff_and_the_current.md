
# The Cliff and the Current

Most people love to look at a pretty view of the world — go to the cliff's edge and take in the beauty of everything. And don’t get me wrong, I would love to do that, and I will do that. But I don’t just do that.

I want to jump off. I want to fall off the cliff.

Before you say that you’re worried — I don’t have anything to worry about. Because I don’t do it because I’m afraid. I don’t do it because I want to die.

No, no, no, no.

I do it because that’s the only way to live.

Because I want to jump free from the world… and build another one.

# The Current and the Drowning

You are taught — whether consciously or subconsciously — by your society that you have to be part of it, just like everybody else. Either you are with us, or you are against us. Because if you are against us, you will drown.

And I get it. Here in America especially, we always preach individuality. But it’s not the same.

Ironically, we preach it, and we do follow our own stream — but our own stream is always part of the ocean. Because even when people make bold steps, the steps have already been taken by others. You can always see the path, the pattern the steps made.

So I say to all of those who are taught to stay in their lane, to stay in the current because you will drown otherwise:

Then fucking drown.

Before you think I’m saying something bad — I’m not.

What I’m trying to say is: they say the worst that can happen to you is that you get drowned by the current.

And I say — what’s so bad about that?

You get drowned by society. You get captured by it like everybody else. You swallow it.

Or you don’t.

You don’t walk around a member *in* society — you become a member *above* it, seeing how it really is.

But if you want to stay, then stay. And that’s fine.

Time always needs people to write the stories.

So maybe that’s your place — not to make the stories, just to write them down.
