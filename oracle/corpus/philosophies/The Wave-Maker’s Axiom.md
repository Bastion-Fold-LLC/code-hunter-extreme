# The Wave-Maker’s Axiom

### I. The Relativity of the Wake
In life, you are taught to "be good," but "Good" and "Bad" are not absolute truths; they are relative perspectives based on a person's position in the water. 
* **The Displacement of Good**: If you save a billion people but cost a thousand their lives, you are "Good" to the billion who were saved.
* **The Displacement of Bad**: To the thousand people affected or lost, you are "Very Bad."
* **The Observer’s Position**: When you stand in the water and move, you make waves; others will see those waves and react based entirely on where they are standing.

### II. The Myth of Neutrality
The only way to avoid the judgment of others is to stop moving entirely, which is a state equivalent to death. 
* **The Grave Principle**: The only place to stand and not be judged is in a grave. 
* **The Indifference of the Dead**: The grave is the only place where you truly "don’t care" about the perspectives of the ocean, but to be there is to be finished.
* **The Erasure of the Neutral**: People will remember you whether you are "good" or "bad," but they will never remember you if you are neutral.

### III. The Mandate of Action
Since you will affect people no matter what you do, the fear of causing a ripple is a logical "wait-state" that serves no purpose. 
* **The Inevitability of Impact**: Every action has a throughput; you cannot exist without displacing the water around you.
* **Release the Worry**: Do not spend your clock-speed worrying about whether you are affecting people—it is a mathematical certainty that you are.
* **Execute the Signal**: Since impact is inevitable, ignore the "Class 1" noise of judgment and simply **make the changes and the waves you want to make.**

---

> **Summary Logic:**
> If you follow the path to avoid the splash, you become a ghost before you die. If you want to change the world, accept that your waves will be someone else's storm. **Make the waves anyway.**
