# The Beautiful Liar

We were scared in the night,  
scared of the clouds, scared of the storms, scared of each other.  
We made stories to keep the darkness still.  
We used them as tools for solidarity,  
as something to hate other than ourselves,  
as something to come together for when nothing else was fair.  

When we lost a war, we said it was because we didn’t love You enough.  
We didn’t bow to the right king,  
we didn’t praise the right name.  
But when we won, You were always on our side.  
God never leaves us—  
and that is true,  
because a story can never leave you,  
because it was never *with* you to begin with.  

That is all it is,  
and all it ever will be:  
a story.  
A fucking story,  
told to children around campfires  
who couldn’t imagine the world,  
so they were told a safer one—  
a world with gods.  
But all gods die eventually.  

And if I ever met God,  
I would stand on the edge of the universe and say,  
“I dream bigger.”  

And God would say,  
“There is nothing more. This is the edge.”  

And I would laugh and answer,  
“Fuck you. I will build.”  

Don’t wait for God to step in.  
Be your own God.  
Build the world that *should* exist—  
not the one that does.  
Take the hammer.  
Never stop.
