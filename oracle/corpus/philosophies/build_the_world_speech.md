# Speech: Build the World

Society generally likes to act like an impatient child, but I will ask you anyway:

**How will you be remembered?**

What if there's no spot in time for what you want to do — or for *you* in general?

If you go quiet, go silent, follow the fucking ranks saying all the nice words, follow the golden brick path until you die — sure, you might be remembered. But generally, you’ll be remembered because of your *path*, not because of *who you are*.

But for those who stepped off the path, **I welcome you**.

For those who say, “I will build,” and when they get told otherwise, they say, “Fuck you, I said I will build.”

For those who found no spots in history, so they *made* the spots exist out of sheer fucking willpower — I welcome you.

If you don’t, you want to stay on your path — I’m sure that’s fine. We need plenty of people to build the path anyway. You can build that path. **We’ll build the world.**

We need each other.

So if you’re not brave enough — I mean *curious* enough — to build everything, just build a little path and walk on that.

Another way to say all of this:  
If you want generations and generations of people in general — or your family or whatever — to say, “Look, that person did their part of being in the path. They were the best cog in the wheel,”  
or do you want them to say, “**Wow...**” and just stop talking for a second out of sheer amazement,  
and then continue saying,  
“That person changed every path.”
