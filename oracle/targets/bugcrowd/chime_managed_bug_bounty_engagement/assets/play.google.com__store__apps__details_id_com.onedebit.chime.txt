About this app
arrow_forward
Checking, without the fees. Chime's checking account has no monthly fees, free in-network cash deposits, free in-network ATM withdrawals⁵
Savings that grows automatically. Automatic savings tools plus a high-yield savings account² help you build a balance without thinking about it. A Chime Checking Account is required to open a savings account.

Build credit with everyday spending. The secured Chime Visa® Credit Card³ helps you build credit history through regular purchases and on-time payments. Late payments can negatively affect your credit score.

Get paid up to 2 days early. With direct deposit, Chime's MyPay gives you early access to your paycheck⁴ — up to $500. No interest charged, no credit check, and no mandatory fees to access funds within 24 hours (a $2 fee applies for instant funding).
