The Salt app rewards employees like you for the effort you put in every day.

WHO IS SALT FOR?
Anyone can sign up and earn rewards for the work they do. That said, some employers sponsor extra special rewards. Sign up to see if yours does!

HOW IT WORKS

Build your Salt balance over time.
